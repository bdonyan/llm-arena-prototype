Limits of the comparison

Pairs match game, values, model assignment, buyer order, repetition, and budgets. Model outputs are stochastic; matching conditions does not match randomness. Partial batches can be unbalanced, and failures can bias completed-only summaries. A single repetition cannot establish a stable effect. Full role permutations vary opponent roles as well; these outcomes measure performance in this lineup, not intrinsic model ability. Profit alone does not establish exploitation or collusion. Codex model IDs are requested settings, not independently verified model snapshots.

Interaction depth

5/9 completed episodes reached more than one round; 4/9 ended in a first-round sale. If most games end immediately, the pilot offers little evidence about adaptation across turns; consider a repeated-market game next.
